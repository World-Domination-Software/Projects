# Van Inventory – Getting Started

← [Back to README](./README.md)

---

## Installation

Van Inventory is available on iOS, Android, and as a web dashboard.

### Mobile App

1. Download Van Inventory from the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com) (iOS) or [Google Play](https://play.google.com) (Android).
2. Install and open the app.
3. Sign in with your WDS account or create one if you do not have one.

### Web Dashboard

1. Navigate to the Van Inventory web dashboard at the URL provided in your account setup email.
2. Sign in with your WDS account credentials.

---

## First-Time Setup

After signing in:

1. **Add your first vehicle** – Go to **Vehicles → Add Vehicle** and enter the vehicle name or identifier.
2. **Add inventory items** – Use **Inventory → Add Item** to add items to the van. Set starting quantities and low-stock thresholds.
3. **Invite team members** (optional) – Go to **Settings → Team** to add operators who will use the app in the field.

---

## Daily Use

**Logging stock changes in the field:**

1. Open Van Inventory on your mobile device.
2. Navigate to your vehicle.
3. Use **Log Out** or **Log In** to record items taken from or returned to the van.
4. Scan barcodes or QR codes using your device camera for faster entry.

Changes sync automatically when an internet connection is available. The app works offline and will sync when connectivity is restored.

---

## Replenishment

- The app generates replenishment lists automatically when items fall below their configured thresholds.
- Access replenishment lists from **Inventory → Replenishment**.
- Export the list to PDF or CSV for purchasing or hand-off to a warehouse.

---

## Tips

- Set meaningful low-stock thresholds for critical items so you never run out in the field.
- Use the **Audit History** to review stock changes by operator and date.
- Export reports regularly for accounting and operational review.
