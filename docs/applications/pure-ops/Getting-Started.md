# Pure OPS – Getting Started

← [Back to README](./README.md)

---

## Installation

Pure OPS is available on PC (Windows and Linux).

1. Purchase or download the game through your chosen storefront (e.g., Steam).
2. Allow the download to complete fully before launching.
3. Launch the game from your platform client or directly from the install directory.

---

## First Launch

On first launch you will be prompted to:

- Choose display and graphics settings.
- Configure control bindings if you prefer non-default inputs.
- Sign in or create a WDS account for online features and progression tracking.

---

## Game Modes

Pure OPS offers two primary modes:

- **Multiplayer** – Play against other players online. Matches are short and objective-driven.
- **Solo / AI** – Play against AI opponents. A good way to learn maps and mechanics before going online.

---

## Basic Gameplay

- Each match has a clearly defined objective shown at the start and visible on the HUD.
- Coordinate movement and cover to execute objectives efficiently.
- Watch your positioning — cover and line-of-sight are critical to success.
- After each match, review the post-match breakdown to see what areas to improve.

---

## Controls

Default controls are shown in the in-game settings menu. All inputs can be rebound.

| Action | Keyboard (Default) | Controller |
|---|---|---|
| Move | WASD | Left Stick |
| Aim | Mouse | Right Stick |
| Fire | Left Click | Right Trigger |
| Aim Down Sights | Right Click | Left Trigger |
| Interact / Use | E | A / Cross |
| Crouch | Ctrl | B / Circle |
| Pause / Menu | Escape | Start |

Full controller support is available.

---

## Tips for New Players

- Start with Solo / AI mode to learn the maps and mechanics before playing online.
- Objectives are time-sensitive — move quickly and communicate with teammates in team modes.
- Use the post-match replay to identify decision points where you could improve.
