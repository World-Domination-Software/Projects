## Concept

Players pilot a spacecraft through procedurally generated star systems, exploring planets, gathering resources, managing ship systems, and surviving the hazards of deep space.

## Core Mechanics

- **Exploration** – Navigate between star systems, survey planets, and scan anomalies.
- **Resource gathering** – Mine asteroids and harvest planetary deposits to fuel upgrades and repairs.
- **Ship management** – Monitor and maintain hull integrity, fuel, life support, and crew status.
- **Survival** – Manage limited resources across long voyages, making decisions that balance risk and reward.
