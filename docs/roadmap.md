# Roadmap

This document outlines the high-level areas we are interested in exploring for our projects.

> **Important:** This roadmap is a plan, not a promise. Priorities and timelines may change based on feedback, technical constraints, and team capacity.

## Possible focus areas

- **Stability and polish** – Fixing crashes, bugs, and rough edges.
- **Performance** – Improving frame rates, load times, and memory usage across platforms (PC, Linux, Steam Deck, and mobile where applicable).
- **Multiplayer experience** – Better matchmaking, connection stability, and social features.
- **Quality-of-life features** – Controls, UI improvements, accessibility options, and small usability tweaks.
- **Content and systems** – New gameplay content and refinements to existing systems.

## How to give feedback

We welcome feedback on our direction:

- Use Discussions for ideas and high-level suggestions.
- Use the **Feature request** issue template for well-defined proposals.

We may update this roadmap over time as projects evolve.
