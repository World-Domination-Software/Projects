# ROADMAP HOW-TO – Following and Using the Roadmap

This post is intended to be pinned in the **Roadmap** discussions category. It explains how we use roadmap topics and how you can follow and respond to them.

## Where is the roadmap?

The **source of truth** for roadmap items is the GitHub Discussions **Roadmap** category:

- Roadmap category: https://github.com/World-Domination-Software/Projects/discussions/categories/roadmap

Each discussion in that category usually represents a theme, milestone, or cluster of related improvements.

## Why use Discussions for the roadmap?

We keep the roadmap in Discussions instead of a static document so that:

- You can comment directly on roadmap topics.
- We can adjust and clarify plans in one visible place.
- Players, testers, and partners can see how ideas evolve over time.

## How to follow roadmap updates

1. Open the **Roadmap** category.
2. Use the **Watch / notification** control on that page to follow the category (for example, *Watching* or *Custom*).
3. Optionally watch the entire repository for broader announcements.
4. Adjust your GitHub notification settings to get email or in-app alerts.

You can always browse the roadmap category manually if you prefer not to enable notifications.

## How to respond to roadmap topics

- Use reactions (👍, ❤️, etc.) for quick feedback.
- Leave comments when you have specific examples, constraints, or use cases.
- Keep feedback constructive and focused on player experience.
- If you have a detailed proposal, you can also open a feature request Issue and link it from the roadmap discussion.

## Important notes

- Roadmap items are **plans, not promises**. Priorities and timelines can change.
- Some work may be exploratory or experimental and might not ship in its initial form.
- We may adjust or consolidate roadmap topics as projects evolve.

For more background, see the wiki page:

- Roadmap overview: https://github.com/World-Domination-Software/Projects/wiki/Roadmap
