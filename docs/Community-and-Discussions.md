# Community and Discussions

GitHub Discussions is the primary place for community interaction on World Domination Software projects.

---

## Where to ask questions

Use [Discussions](https://github.com/World-Domination-Software/Projects/discussions) when you:

- Need help troubleshooting but are not yet sure it is a bug.
- Have questions about features, platforms, or settings.
- Want to share general feedback.

Before opening a new discussion, search existing topics by keyword — your question may already be answered.

---

## Where to share ideas

Use the **Ideas** or **Feature Requests** discussion category to propose new features or improvements. Describe the problem you want solved and how your idea addresses it. The team and community can react and comment.

---

## Where to view the roadmap

Roadmap planning and discussion happens in the [Roadmap category](https://github.com/World-Domination-Software/Projects/discussions/categories/roadmap).

Each roadmap discussion represents a theme or cluster of related improvements. You can:

- React with emojis to signal which areas matter most to you.
- Comment with specific examples, use cases, or concerns.
- Follow the category to receive notifications about new posts.

---

## Where to report bugs

Use [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/World-Domination-Software/Projects/issues) for clear, reproducible bugs. See [[Bug-Reporting]] for what to include.

If you are unsure whether something is a bug or a question, start in Discussions. Maintainers can move it to an Issue if needed.

---

## How to participate professionally

- Keep discussions on topic and constructive.
- Be respectful of other community members and maintainers.
- Provide enough context for others to understand and respond helpfully.
- Avoid duplicate posts — search first.
- Follow the project [Code of Conduct](https://github.com/World-Domination-Software/Projects/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
