# Roadkill – Gameplay Guide

← Back to [[Roadkill]]

---

## Game Modes

Roadkill offers several distinct modes:

| Mode | Description |
|---|---|
| **Race** | First to complete the required laps or reach the finish line wins. |
| **Elimination** | The last-place vehicle is eliminated at intervals until one remains. |
| **Last Vehicle Standing** | Destroy all opposing vehicles to win. |
| **Objective** | Complete a defined objective (e.g., capture zones, collect items) before your opponents. |

Modes are available in both local multiplayer and online play.

---

## Vehicles

Each vehicle class has distinct characteristics:

- **Light / Nimble** – High speed and acceleration, low armor. Best for race modes and evasion.
- **Medium / Balanced** – Good all-around performance for mixed modes.
- **Heavy / Combat Rig** – High armor and firepower, lower top speed. Dominant in elimination and last-vehicle-standing.

New vehicles and skins are unlocked through play and progression.

## Controls

| Action | Keyboard (Default) | Controller |
|---|---|---|
| Accelerate | W / Up Arrow | Right Trigger |
| Brake / Reverse | S / Down Arrow | Left Trigger |
| Steer | A/D / Left/Right Arrow | Left Stick |
| Weapon / Attack | Space / Left Click | Right Bumper |
| Handbrake | Left Shift | Left Bumper |
| Camera | Mouse / Right Stick | Right Stick |

Controls can be fully rebound in the in-game settings menu.

---

## Progression

- **Earn currency** through match performance — place higher, score more.
- **Unlock vehicles** from the garage using earned currency.
- **Unlock skins and cosmetics** through play milestones and events.
- **Leaderboards** track your performance per track and mode.

---

## Multiplayer

### Online

- Online matches are hosted on WDS dedicated servers.
- Use the **Quick Play** lobby for casual matches, or invite friends to a private lobby.
- All players must be on the same game version.

### Local Multiplayer

- Local play supports split-screen on supported display configurations.
- Connect controllers before launching a local session.

---

## Tips

- Learn the weapon timing for each vehicle class — heavy rigs deal more damage but have slower fire rates.
- In race modes, use the handbrake for tight corners rather than braking fully.
- In elimination modes, hanging back briefly can let other players eliminate each other early.
