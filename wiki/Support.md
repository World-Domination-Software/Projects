## Tips for Getting Help Quickly

- **Include your platform and version** in any support request (Windows/Linux/iOS/etc., and the build number shown in the app or game menu).
- **Describe what you tried** — if you already followed steps from [[Troubleshooting-Index]] or [[FAQ]], mention that so we can skip straight to deeper investigation.
- **Search first** — browse open and closed [issues](https://github.com/World-Domination-Software/Projects/issues) and [discussions](https://github.com/World-Domination-Software/Projects/discussions) before posting. Your question may already have an answer.
