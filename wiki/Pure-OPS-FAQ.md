## Gameplay

### Is Pure OPS single-player only, or does it have multiplayer?

Both. Pure OPS supports online multiplayer (with dedicated servers), and solo modes against AI opponents.

### How long does a typical match last?

Matches are short by design — usually a few minutes, depending on the mission type and how quickly objectives are completed. The focus is on quick, repeatable sessions.

### Is there a progression system?

Yes. You earn unlockable loadouts and cosmetic items through play. Progression carries between matches.

### Are there ranked or competitive modes?

Ranked matchmaking is currently in development. Quick play is available now.

### What are the platform requirements?

See the product page on your platform or storefront for minimum and recommended hardware.

### Is there controller support?

Yes — PC supports Xbox-style and PlayStation-style controllers. Steam Deck support is planned.

## Multiplayer

### Why can't I connect to a match?

See [[Pure-OPS-Troubleshooting]] for a step-by-step guide to common connection issues.

### Do I need a fast internet connection?

A stable connection is more important than raw speed. Lower your in-game latency settings or try a wired Ethernet connection if you experience lag.

### Can I play solo offline?

Yes. Solo modes run locally against AI and do not require an internet connection.
