## Gameplay

### What game modes are available?

Race, Elimination, Last Vehicle Standing, and Objective-based modes. See [[Roadkill-Gameplay]] for details on each.

### How many players can play at once?

Online multiplayer and local multiplayer (split-screen) are both supported. The exact player count per mode is listed in the lobby or mode selection screen.

### How do I unlock new vehicles?

Vehicles are unlocked by earning in-game currency through match performance. Currency can be spent in the garage to unlock vehicles and cosmetics.

### Is there a story mode?

Roadkill is focused on arcade-style competitive play rather than a narrative campaign. Single-player content is available in the form of AI races and challenge modes.

### What are the platform requirements?

See the product page on your platform or storefront for minimum and recommended hardware specifications.

### Is there controller support?

Yes — full controller support for Xbox-style and PlayStation-style controllers on PC. Steam Deck also supports the game with full controller input.

## Multiplayer

### Can I play with friends online?

Yes. Use a private lobby to invite friends, or use Quick Play for open matchmaking.

### Can I play locally with a friend?

Yes. Local split-screen multiplayer is supported with multiple controllers.

### Why am I getting lag or rubber-banding?

This is usually caused by an unstable internet connection. See [[Roadkill-Troubleshooting]] for steps to diagnose and improve connection quality.
