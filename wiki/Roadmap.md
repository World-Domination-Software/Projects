# Roadmap

Our roadmap is hosted publicly in GitHub Discussions so that the community can follow progress, comment on priorities, and react to planned work.

➡️ **[View the Roadmap Discussions](https://github.com/World-Domination-Software/Projects/discussions/categories/roadmap)**

---

## How It Works

> **Note:** The roadmap reflects our current intentions and focus areas. Priorities and timelines may change based on feedback, technical constraints, and team capacity. It is a plan, not a binding commitment.

Each roadmap discussion thread represents a theme, milestone, or cluster of related improvements. You can:

- **Comment** with specific examples, use cases, or concerns.
- **React** with emojis to show which areas matter most to you.
- **Follow** individual threads to receive notifications when they are updated.

## Current Focus Areas

The roadmap discussion covers themes including:

- **Stability and polish** – Fixing crashes, bugs, and rough edges across all projects.
- **Performance** – Frame rates, load times, and memory usage (PC, Linux, Steam Deck, mobile).
- **Multiplayer experience** – Matchmaking, connection stability, and social features.
- **Quality-of-life** – Controls, UI improvements, accessibility, and small usability tweaks.
- **Content and systems** – New gameplay content and refinements to existing systems.

Visit the roadmap discussions for the latest detail on each theme.

## How to Give Feedback

We welcome community input on direction:

- **Comment on roadmap threads** for high-level reactions and specific suggestions.
- **Use the Feature request** issue template for well-defined, actionable proposals.
- **React** to roadmap posts with emoji so we can gauge relative interest across themes.
